The logged-in shelf, at two sizes.

Both frames are the live pages, not pictures: the same markup, the same filters, the same archive. The rail is the web; the five-slot bar at the bottom is the phone, which is also the shape the Android and iOS builds use.

Web · from 1280 up

Phone · 390 × 844

What changes on the phone

  • The rail becomes a five-slot bar: Library, Search, Add in the middle, Topics, You.
  • A save becomes a card — source, title, three lines, then ref and actions on their own rule.
  • Search leaves the bar and drops out of it on tap; a field beside the brand does not survive 390.
  • The month stays the only grouping. There is still no ranking to explain.
  • One type scale either way: 27/15.5/13 on the web, 24/16/13 on the phone.
  • Nothing is hidden behind a hamburger, and the tap targets are 44px tall.

The web frame follows the site's own breakpoints; at 900 the rail is replaced by the bar, so narrowing this window is the same test as opening it on a phone. The reading view is its own page.
