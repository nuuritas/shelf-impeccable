Notes

What the shelf holds

  1. 01Seven kinds of save, one card: an X post, an X article, a LinkedIn post, a video, a reel, a web page, an agent's artifact.
  2. 02The card carries what the source gave it — the paragraph, the picture, the player's still, the document's opening.
  3. 03The AI summary is a band inside the card, two lines, folding with the text it reads.
  4. 04A tap folds the card. The only screens it opens are the two a card cannot be: a reel and an artifact.
  5. 05Archive, copy and the source live inside the open card, so a folded shelf is all content.
  6. 06Chrome is one bar and one row of four menus; the row scrolls away, and nothing is pinned.

Reading this sheet

The web frame follows the site's own breakpoints; at 900 the rail is replaced by the phone bar, so narrowing this window is the same test as opening it on a phone. The artifact is its own page, and the reel is the source's.
