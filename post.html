The quiet return of RSS

Feeds never died; they moved back into readers, and a small chronological list is winning again.

Summarywritten by the managed key

RSS was not beaten by a better product, it was left out of the one everybody opens. The case here is for a list you own: a feed you picked, a reader that keeps its place, and no ranking deciding what comes next. Small and chronological is the point, not the compromise — a shelf you chose beats a feed that chose you.
